Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information Forest App collects and how it is used. The App is operated by Vikas justa.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, username and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile details you choose to add: date of birth, designation, usual tour start point, beat name, forest block, forest range and a profile picture.
  • Subscription records: the plan you bought, payment reference IDs and validity dates. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach our servers.
  • On your device: your diary entries and the photos you capture are stored on your phone. The App uses the camera, location and photo library permissions only to take GPS-stamped photos and save them for you.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and to send verification and password reset emails.
  • To activate and manage your subscription.
  • To show your profile inside the App.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the service: Razorpay (payments), Cloudinary (profile picture storage), Brevo (transactional email) and cloud hosting and database providers. They receive only what they need to perform their function.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. To have your account and associated data deleted, email justpahadi.hp@gmail.com from your registered address. Payment records may be retained as required by law.

Security

We use hashed passwords, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

The App is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

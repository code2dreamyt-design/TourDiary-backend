Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Cancellation

Subscriptions are prepaid for one period (30 days or 365 days) and do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel and no future charge will be made unless you choose to buy again. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

If your payment was successful but your subscription was not activated within 48 hours of the payment, you are entitled to a full refund of that payment.

Apart from this case, payments for a subscription that has been delivered are not refundable.

How to request a refund

Email justpahadi.hp@gmail.com from your registered email address with the payment reference (the Razorpay payment ID shown in your payment confirmation) and the date of payment. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once we initiate a refund, it usually reaches your account in 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Duplicate payments

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, contact us and the extra payment will be refunded.

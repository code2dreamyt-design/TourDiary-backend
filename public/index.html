Forest App

A field-work diary app for forest staff such as Van Mitras, Forest Guards and Forest Workers. Keep daily tour entries, attach GPS-stamped photos, and export a completed month as a Word document.

What you get

  • Monthly tour diary with daily From / To / Remarks entries
  • Camera with GPS and time stamped photos
  • Word (.docx) export of a completed month
  • Your profile, saved to your account

Everything in one app

Made for the way forest work really goes

No paper registers, no lost photos, no last-minute rewriting. Every feature exists because field staff asked for it.

Monthly tour diary

Month-end. The inspection is tomorrow and the diary is blank. You sit with a pen trying to remember where you were on the 12th, the 19th, the 23rd, and hope nothing contradicts.

Write each day while it is fresh, in about 30 seconds. This month's diary is created for you the first time you open it, with one empty entry for every day.

How it works

  • Fill in From, To and Remarks for the day
  • Edit any entry later, it updates in place and never duplicates
  • Missed a day? It stays empty and visible until you fill it
  • A progress bar and completion % show how far each month is

Why it matters. Entries written the same day are accurate. You see on the 15th, not the 30th, that you are behind. Month-end becomes a download, not a panic.

GPS & time-stamped camera

You saw the illegal felling and took a photo. Weeks later someone asks where exactly, and when. A plain photo proves nothing.

Latitude, longitude, elevation, GPS accuracy and time are burned onto the photo itself, so the proof can never be separated from the picture.

How it works

  • Live preview shows your position, with accuracy coloured green, yellow or red
  • Add an optional note that is stamped on the photo
  • Retake instantly, or tick Add to today's entry to attach it to your diary
  • Saved straight to your phone's Gallery

Why it matters. Your remarks and your photo now back each other up, with the evidence inside the image.

Today's entry on Home New

The diary is somewhere inside the app and you are tired after a long patrol. You say tomorrow, and tomorrow becomes a week.

Today's entry is the first thing on Home, right under this month's progress. Your usual starting place is already filled in as From.

How it works

  • Open the app and today's card is there
  • Add To and Remarks, tap Save Today's Entry
  • Future dates stay locked, so each day is written on its day

Why it matters. One tap from opening the app to writing. Less effort means fewer missed days.

Daily diary reminder New

Nobody forgets on purpose. Three busy days pass and on the fourth you realise nothing is written for any of them.

The app nudges you at the time you choose, on the days you choose. If today's entry is already done, it stays quiet.

How it works

  • Open Notebook, then Reminder Settings
  • Pick a time (6:00 PM by default) and the weekdays
  • Leave Skip if done on and filling the entry cancels the nudge

Why it matters. It runs on the phone itself, so it works with no signal. The habit builds without effort.

One-tap Word export

Month-end means retyping the whole diary into the department format and hunting for a printer. Hours spent redoing work you already did.

A finished .docx in the department's layout, with title, dated table and the right signature block for your designation.

How it works

  • Fill every day until the diary reaches 100%
  • Tap Download Word on the diary or its card
  • The first time, pick a folder. After that it saves there by itself

Why it matters. Van Mitras and Forest Workers get Forest Guard I/C, Block Officer and Range Officer lines. Forest Guards get Block Officer and Range Officer. Print and sign.

Observations New

A leopard pugmark, a snare, fresh stumps. You saw it on patrol, noted it on a scrap of paper, and by the time someone asks, the details are gone.

Record what you saw with its place, time and photos, so every sighting becomes a proper record you can search later.

How it works

  • Choose a category: wildlife sighting, animal signs, illegal felling, poaching or snare, forest fire, encroachment, water source, plantation, pest or disease
  • Capture the GPS position with one tap, or type the place
  • Attach up to 6 photos from the stamped camera or your Gallery
  • Mark important ones so they stay on top

Why it matters. Sightings and offences are written down on the spot, with proof, not remembered later.

Reminders New

A group patrol, a call from the DFO, a court summons, an order to complete. You are busy in the field, and the one task that mattered slips your mind.

Reminders are for anything important that you do not want to forget, not only the diary. Write what it is, set the time, and the phone reminds you.

How it works

  • Write the task, and pick the time, or tap a quick choice like In 1 hour or Tomorrow 9 AM
  • Repeat it once, daily, weekly or monthly
  • Get early alerts from 10 minutes up to 2 days before, plus an alert at the exact time
  • Keep reminding me: up to 4 follow-ups until you mark it done
  • Not now? Snooze for 10 minutes, 1 hour or until tomorrow 9 AM

Why it matters. No task, order or meeting slips. It works on the phone itself, so alerts arrive with no signal.

Notes New

A phone number, a measurement, an instruction from the Range Officer. It lives in a chat or on your hand, and is hard to find again.

Quick notes for anything you want to keep, with search so you can find them in seconds.

How it works

  • Add a title and your note
  • Colour-tag it Green, Gold, Blue, Red or Purple
  • Pin the important ones to the top

Why it matters. Small facts stay in one place instead of scattered across chats and paper.

TD calculator

Volumes by hand, girth classes from a table, a conversion limit to check, and one rounding mistake on a record that will be audited.

Record TD and seized timber in three steps. Volumes appear as you type, and the app tells you when you cross the 65% conversion limit.

How it works

  • Choose TD (with marking number and paid or free-grant status) or Seized timber
  • Pick up to 4 trees from Kail, Deodar, Rai, Tosh and Chil, with their girth class
  • Add length, width, thickness and quantity for each size

Why it matters. The right numbers every time, in the format the permits use.

Exact timber arithmetic

Two people add the same sizes and get different totals, because of when they rounded.

The app works in whole millimetres and exact 3-decimal volumes, so there is no drift and every row total is correct.

How it works

  • Row totals and conversion % are calculated for you
  • Recent compartments are offered as one-tap suggestions
  • Export a single record, or a whole batch by date range, as Word

Why it matters. Records match every time, and a whole month of TD paperwork exports in one go.

Android install guide

Install Forest App in 4 easy steps

The app is installed directly from an APK file. Follow these steps once and you're done.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap Download Forest App (or scan the QR code from another device). Your phone saves Forest.App.apk in Downloads.

  2. Allow installation from this source

    When Android asks, open Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser or Files app. You can switch it off again afterwards.

  3. Open the file and tap Install

    Open Forest.App.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder and tap Install, then Open.

  4. Sign up and allow permissions

    Create your account, verify your email, and allow Camera, Location and Photos access when the app asks, so GPS-stamped photos work.

Security prompt? Because the app is installed from an APK and not from the Play Store, Android or Play Protect may show a warning. If you downloaded it from this page, choose More details → Install anyway.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?
Your diary entries and photos are stored on your phone, so you can keep working with weak or no signal in the forest. You need internet to sign up, verify your email and make payments.
How do I claim the 30-day free offer?
Install the app and sign up before the offer ends. Open the verification link we email you, then tap Claim on the offer banner at the top of the Home screen. Your free month starts right away. If you haven't verified your email yet, the app will remind you.
Why does my phone show a security warning?
The app is installed from an APK file rather than the Play Store, so Android may ask you to confirm. If you downloaded it from this page, choose Install anyway. Only download the APK from this official page.
What happens if my subscription ends?
Everything you saved stays on your phone and you can still view it. To create, edit or export entries and records you need an active subscription. Renewing adds time on top of what you have left.
Which permissions does the app need, and why?
Camera for tour photos, Location while the app is in use to stamp GPS coordinates, and Photos / Media access to save pictures to your Gallery. If you decline, the app shows a simple screen asking for access instead of breaking.
Is there an iPhone version?
The download on this page is for Android phones. An iPhone version is not available from this page.
How do refunds and cancellation work?
Payments are one-time for the chosen period and never renew automatically. Full details are in our Cancellation & Refund Policy, and you can reach us any time from the Contact page.

Android install guide

Install Forest App in 4 easy steps

The app is installed directly from an APK file. Follow these steps once and you're done.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap Download Forest App (or scan the QR code from another device). Your phone saves ForestApp.apk in Downloads.

  2. Allow installation from this source

    When Android asks, open Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser or Files app. You can switch it off again afterwards.

  3. Open the file and tap Install

    Open ForestApp.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder and tap Install, then Open.

  4. Sign up and allow permissions

    Create your account, verify your email, and allow Camera, Location and Photos access when the app asks, so GPS-stamped photos work.

Security prompt? Because the app is installed from an APK and not from the Play Store, Android or Play Protect may show a warning. If you downloaded it from this page, choose More details → Install anyway.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?
Your diary entries and photos are stored on your phone, so you can keep working with weak or no signal in the forest. You need internet to sign up, verify your email and make payments.
How do I claim the 30-day free offer?
Install the app and sign up before the offer ends. Open the verification link we email you, then tap Claim on the offer banner at the top of the Home screen. Your free month starts right away. If you haven't verified your email yet, the app will remind you.
Why does my phone show a security warning?
The app is installed from an APK file rather than the Play Store, so Android may ask you to confirm. If you downloaded it from this page, choose Install anyway. Only download the APK from this official page.
What happens if my subscription ends?
Everything you saved stays on your phone and you can still view it. To create, edit or export entries and records you need an active subscription. Renewing adds time on top of what you have left.
Which permissions does the app need, and why?
Camera for tour photos, Location while the app is in use to stamp GPS coordinates, and Photos / Media access to save pictures to your Gallery. If you decline, the app shows a simple screen asking for access instead of breaking.
Is there an iPhone version?
The download on this page is for Android phones. An iPhone version is not available from this page.
How do refunds and cancellation work?
Payments are one-time for the chosen period and never renew automatically. Full details are in our Cancellation & Refund Policy, and you can reach us any time from the Contact page.
